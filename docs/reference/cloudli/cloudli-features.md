# Cloudli Business Communication Features

Cloudli offers a comprehensive suite of cloud-based communications, VoIP, and specialized fax solutions. Below is a breakdown of their core features and capabilities.

---

## 1. Cloudli Connect (Hosted Business Phone System)

The primary VoIP service designed to replace traditional PBX systems with cloud-based flexibility.

* **Core Infrastructure:**
  * **Hosted IP Telephony:** Fully managed cloud infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring.
  * **Online Admin Portal:** Web-based dashboard to manage users, extensions, and billing.
  * **HD Voice Quality:** High-performance audio codecs for clear communication.
  * **Interactive Voice Response (IVR):** Professional automated menus (e.g., "Press 1 for Sales").
  * **REST API:** Integration support for CRMs and third-party applications.

* **Advanced Call Management:**
  * **Call Routing & Forwarding:** Smart redirection to mobile, desktop, or other extensions.
  * **Enhanced Voicemail:** Access messages via portal, mobile, or as email/SMS notifications.
  * **Call Queues & Group Calls:** Manage high-volume incoming traffic with virtual wait lines.
  * **Call Parking:** "Park" a call on a unique number for retrieval by any team member.
  * **Presence Indication (BLF):** Visual status lights showing if a colleague is on a call or available.
  * **Call Screening:** Intercept and filter unwanted calls or restrict international dialing.

---

## 2. Mobile & Desktop Applications

Enabling a "work from anywhere" environment across iOS, Android, Windows, and Mac.

* **Softphones:** Full business phone functionality on your personal or company laptop/mobile.
* **Business SMS/MMS:** Send and receive texts using your professional business number.
* **Team Messaging:** Internal chat, rich media sharing, and group collaboration.
* **Contact Sync:** Integration with Microsoft 365 and local contact lists.

---

## 3. Contact Centre & Supervision

Advanced tools for managing customer-facing teams and service quality.

* **Real-Time Dashboards:** Visual metrics for call volumes, wait times, and agent productivity.
* **Smart Queuing:** Skills-based routing (routing callers by language, expertise, or location).
* **Agent Supervision:**
  * **Monitor:** Listen to live calls.
  * **Whisper:** Coach the agent without the caller hearing.
  * **Barge-in:** Take over the call if necessary.
* **Detailed Analytics:** Out-of-the-box reports on agent performance and customer service history.

---

## 4. IP Fax (FoIP) Solutions

Cloudli is a market leader in "Fax-over-IP," focusing on reliability and security.

* **Virtual Fax:** Send and receive faxes directly via email or the Cloudli portal.
* **T.38 Fax SIP Trunks:** High-reliability connectivity for existing physical fax machines.
* **Security & Compliance:** Encrypted faxing options suitable for healthcare (HIPAA) and legal sectors.

---

## 5. Connectivity & Managed Services

* **SIP Trunking:** Modernize on-premises PBX systems with cloud connectivity.
* **Business Internet:** Dedicated high-speed internet and 5G/4G fixed wireless backup.
* **SD-WAN Lite:** Optimized routing to ensure voice and fax traffic prioritize quality.
* **Mass Notifications:** Automated tools for appointment reminders, emergency alerts, and community broadcasts.

---

## 6. Global Presence & Numbers

* **Local & Toll-Free:** Numbers available across thousands of area codes in Canada and the U.S.
* **International Coverage:** Reach into 76 countries with local virtual numbers.
* **Number Porting:** Full support for transferring existing business numbers to the Cloudli platform.
